6. Relationship with Intuit and QuickBooks

The Software is a client of the Intuit QuickBooks Online Accounting API. It requests a single OAuth scope, com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting. Intuit does not offer a variant of that scope limited to reading data; consequently the Software is technically capable of creating, updating, voiding and deleting accounting records in any QuickBooks company connected to it.

The use of QuickBooks is governed by the agreement between the owner of each QuickBooks company and Intuit Inc., which is separate from and unaffected by this Agreement. Licensor does not control QuickBooks, does not guarantee its availability, and is not responsible for any act, omission, outage, data loss or change of interface on the part of Intuit.

The connection between the Software and a QuickBooks company may be ended at any time, either from within the Software or by disconnecting the application from the list of connected apps in the settings of that QuickBooks Online company. Disconnection revokes the Software's access to that company; it does not delete records already stored in QuickBooks.

7. Data

The Software runs locally on equipment controlled by Licensee. It operates without any server, backend or database maintained by Licensor. The Software's own code opens network connections only to Intuit endpoints and to the content delivery hosts from which Intuit serves QuickBooks attachments, and it contains no analytics, telemetry or error-reporting component.

The Software's Model Context Protocol server returns Company Data to the local client program that invokes it. Licensee operates the Software through third-party client software, under the direct instruction of authorized personnel of Evestar Group LLC. Company Data retrieved through that client software is transmitted to, and processed by, the third-party service provider that operates it, subject to that provider's commercial terms. This processing is described in the Privacy Policy published alongside this Agreement.

The Software processes Company Data, which includes contact information of customers, vendors and employees (name, business name, email address, telephone number, and billing and shipping address) together with financial amounts. The Software does not request payment card numbers, bank account or routing numbers, or Social Security Numbers, and has no function dedicated to storing them.

OAuth tokens, local audit logs and any files downloaded through the Software are written to equipment controlled by Licensee, where the client software described above may also keep its own history of the sessions in which the Software was used. Licensee is solely responsible for the physical and logical security of that equipment, including operating system account security, screen locking and backup. Details of how the Software stores credentials and what it records locally are described in the Privacy Policy published alongside this Agreement.
