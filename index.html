Evestar Accounting Bridge

A private accounting integration operated by Evestar Group LLC

What this is

Evestar Accounting Bridge is an internal software tool operated by Evestar Group LLC, a digital marketing agency and operating company of the Evestar group operating in Florida, the United States. Its sole purpose is to let authorized personnel of Evestar Group LLC work with the QuickBooks Online companies of Evestar Group and of its affiliated companies, through the Intuit QuickBooks Online Accounting API.

What this is not

  • It is not distributed to anyone and it is not for sale.
  • It is not listed on the Intuit App Store or on any other marketplace.
  • It has no external users: there is no sign-up, no customer account and no user base. Only authorized personnel of Evestar Group LLC operate it.

Why there is nothing to launch here

The application is a desktop tool (a command-line interface and a local automation server) that runs on an authorized computer. It has no web interface, so there is nothing to open or sign in to from this website. This page exists to identify the application and to host its legal documents, which Intuit requires to be publicly reachable.
